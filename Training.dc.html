What students will learn

01

Dog Handling & Salon Fundamentals

Safe handling, understanding behaviour, salon routines, equipment, hygiene and how a professional grooming salon operates.

02

Bathing, Drying & Coat Preparation

How dogs are prepared properly for a groom, including bathing, product selection, drying, brushing and coat preparation.

03

Clippers & Grooming Techniques

An introduction to clippers, blades, comb attachments, hygiene areas, paws, nails, ears and the practical techniques used during a groom.

04

Scissoring, Styling & Finishing

Shadowing the team through shaping, scissoring and finishing, and understanding what creates a balanced, professional-looking groom.

05

The Complete Grooming Journey

Seeing how it all comes together – from a dog arriving at reception through consultation, preparation, grooming, finishing and collection.

How the course works

How the course works

This is not just a course about grooming – it is an opportunity to experience what working in a professional grooming salon is actually like.

Students pitch in, observe real grooms, ask questions and learn from experienced people at every stage. With a maximum of two students, it stays personal and hands-on.

Certificate of completion

This is an introductory practical course with an in-house certificate. It does not qualify anyone as a professional dog groomer or represent an externally accredited qualification.

Students who complete the five-day course receive an in-house Zoomies Academy Certificate of Completion.
