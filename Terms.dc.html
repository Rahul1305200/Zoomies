Customer Terms of Service

Effective 12 October 2026 | Zoomies Limited

These Terms of Service apply to grooming, small-dog daycare and related pet-care services supplied by Zoomies Limited, trading as Zoomies (“Zoomies”, “we”, “us” or “our”). Business address: 116A Lower Kilmacud Road, Stillorgan, South County Dublin. Email: hello@zoomies.ie. By booking a service, creating a customer account or leaving a dog in our care, the customer confirms that they own the dog or are authorised by its owner to accept these Terms.

1. Owner information and responsibilities

The customer must provide complete and accurate information about their dog, including:

  • Age, breed and sex;
  • Vaccination status;
  • Medical conditions, allergies and medication;
  • Previous injuries or surgeries;
  • Pregnancy or possible pregnancy;
  • Behavioural concerns;
  • History of aggression, biting, snapping, resource guarding or reactivity;
  • Sensitivity to handling, dryers, clippers, water, other dogs or confinement; and
  • Any other information reasonably relevant to the dog’s safety or welfare.

The customer must immediately inform Zoomies if any of this information changes. Failure to disclose relevant medical or behavioural information may result in the service being stopped, the dog being refused future services and the customer being responsible for reasonable losses, veterinary costs or other expenses directly caused by the omission.

2. Bookings and cancellations

Appointments are subject to availability and are only confirmed when accepted by Zoomies. As appointments involve reserved staff time and capacity:

  • Cancellations or changes made more than 48 hours before the appointment will not normally incur a cancellation charge.
  • Cancellations or changes made within 48 hours but more than 24 hours before the appointment may incur a charge equal to 50% of the booked service price.
  • Cancellations or changes made within 24 hours of the appointment, same-day cancellations and failures to attend may incur a charge equal to 100% of the booked service price.

A cancellation is effective when it is received and acknowledged by Zoomies through an approved contact method. Zoomies may waive or reduce a cancellation charge at its discretion in exceptional circumstances or where we successfully fill the cancelled appointment. Any outstanding cancellation charge may need to be paid before another appointment is accepted. These charges are intended to compensate Zoomies for staff time and appointment capacity reserved for the customer. They are not intended to operate as a punishment.

3. Late arrival

Customers should arrive at the agreed time. Late arrival may result in:

  • A shortened service;
  • Part of the booked service being omitted;
  • The appointment being rescheduled; or
  • The appointment being treated as a late cancellation.

Where an appointment cannot reasonably proceed because of late arrival, the applicable cancellation charge may be applied.

4. Collection and late collection

Dogs must be collected promptly when the customer is informed that the service has finished or by the agreed daycare closing time. A reasonable late-collection fee may apply where a dog is not collected at the agreed time. Repeated late collection may result in future bookings being refused. Where a customer cannot be contacted, Zoomies may contact the nominated emergency contact.

5. Prices and payment

Prices may vary based on the dog’s:

  • Breed, size and coat type;
  • Coat condition;
  • Behaviour;
  • Time required;
  • Grooming history; and
  • Services actually required.

Prices quoted before physically assessing the dog are estimates unless Zoomies expressly confirms otherwise. Additional charges may apply for severe matting, extended handling time, behavioural difficulties, parasite treatment, excessive coat condition or other work beyond the service originally booked. Payment is due when requested and, unless otherwise agreed, before the dog leaves the premises.

6. Fleas

Customers must not bring a dog to Zoomies if they know or reasonably suspect that the dog has fleas. If fleas are identified before grooming begins, Zoomies may refuse to proceed with the booked groom. If fleas are discovered after the dog has entered the grooming area: 1. The planned groom will normally stop; 2. Zoomies may provide an immediate medicated flea bath where it is safe and appropriate to do so; 3. The dog will be separated from other animals as far as reasonably practicable; 4. The customer will be contacted and required to arrange prompt collection; and 5. A €60 flea-treatment charge will apply. The flea-treatment charge covers the medicated bath, additional handling and the cleaning and disinfection measures required following exposure. The flea bath is an immediate control measure and is not a substitute for veterinary advice or an appropriate home flea-treatment programme. The customer remains responsible for arranging any further treatment required for the dog, the home and other animals in the household. Zoomies does not guarantee that one flea bath will eliminate every flea, egg or larva.

7. Ticks and other parasites

Customers must inform Zoomies of any known or suspected ticks, mites, lice, worms, skin infections or other parasites. Where a tick is discovered, Zoomies may:

  • Notify the customer;
  • Avoid disturbing the tick;
  • Recommend removal or examination by a veterinary professional; or
  • Remove the tick where the customer has authorised this and a suitably trained team member considers it safe.

Tick removal is not veterinary treatment. A tick’s mouthparts can occasionally remain in the skin even where reasonable care is taken. The customer should monitor the area and obtain veterinary advice if there is swelling, redness, discharge, illness or any other concern. Zoomies may stop or refuse a service where parasites create a health, welfare or cross-contamination risk.

8. Infectious illness

Dogs must not attend Zoomies if they:

  • Have vomiting, diarrhoea, coughing or unusual nasal discharge;
  • Have a suspected contagious condition;
  • Have been advised to isolate by a veterinary professional;
  • Have recently been exposed to a serious contagious illness; or
  • Are otherwise unwell and unsuitable for group care or grooming.

The customer must notify Zoomies as soon as possible if their dog develops a contagious illness shortly after attending. Zoomies may refuse entry, isolate a dog temporarily or require immediate collection where illness is suspected. While Zoomies takes reasonable hygiene and infection-control precautions, no shared animal environment can be guaranteed to be completely free from communicable illness.

9. Daycare vaccinations

Dogs attending daycare must have current vaccinations appropriate to group daycare, including:

  • DHPPI, or the equivalent vaccination protocol advised by the dog’s veterinary professional; and
  • Kennel cough vaccination.

Customers must provide acceptable vaccination evidence before attendance and whenever requested. Zoomies may also require evidence of any additional vaccination or veterinary assessment reasonably considered necessary to protect the dogs in its care. Vaccination reduces risk but does not completely prevent infection. Customers acknowledge that dogs attending group daycare may still be exposed to communicable illnesses despite appropriate precautions.

10. Daycare assessment and suitability

Daycare is a social group environment and is not suitable for every dog. Before or during attendance, Zoomies may assess the dog’s:

  • Behaviour around people and dogs;
  • Stress levels;
  • Play style;
  • Ability to respond to staff intervention;
  • Resource guarding;
  • Physical condition; and
  • General suitability for group care.

Acceptance for an assessment or previous daycare attendance does not guarantee permanent acceptance. Zoomies may separate, suspend or permanently exclude a dog where reasonably necessary to protect that dog, another dog, staff members or customers. A customer must disclose all known incidents of aggression, biting, fighting, reactivity or serious anxiety, whether they occurred at home, in public or in another pet-care facility.

11. Female dogs and dogs in season

Female dogs aged over 12 months must be spayed to attend daycare unless Zoomies agrees otherwise in writing following consideration of the individual circumstances. An unspayed female dog under 12 months must not attend while she is:

  • In season or suspected to be in season;
  • Showing signs that a season is beginning;
  • Pregnant or potentially pregnant; or
  • Within a period following her season during which Zoomies reasonably considers group attendance unsuitable.

The customer is responsible for monitoring the dog and informing Zoomies of any signs of a season. Zoomies may refuse entry or require immediate collection if it believes that a dog is in season. Zoomies will take reasonable care but cannot guarantee that every early or subtle sign of a season will be identified. Where the customer has failed to disclose signs that the dog may be in season, the customer accepts the inherent risk of interaction with male dogs. Nothing in this clause excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded.

12. Male dogs

Zoomies may assess unneutered male dogs individually. An unneutered male may be refused, separated or excluded if hormonal, mounting, marking, guarding, aggressive or disruptive behaviour makes group daycare unsuitable.

13. Risks associated with daycare

Daycare includes interaction between live animals. Even with supervision, appropriate grouping and reasonable care, normal play can involve:

  • Running and jumping;
  • Physical contact;
  • Scratches or minor cuts;
  • Strains or sprains;
  • Accidental collisions;
  • Disagreements between dogs;
  • Tiredness;
  • Damage to collars, harnesses or personal belongings; and
  • Exposure to communicable illness.

Customers acknowledge these inherent and unavoidable risks. Zoomies will take reasonable care in supervising dogs and responding to incidents. This acknowledgment does not excuse Zoomies from liability where loss or injury is caused by negligence or a failure to provide the service with the standard required by law.

14. Grooming consent

The customer authorises Zoomies to groom the dog using the techniques, equipment and products reasonably considered suitable for the booked service and the dog’s condition. Grooming involves a live animal whose behaviour can be unpredictable. Dogs may move suddenly, resist handling, bite, scratch or react to clippers, scissors, dryers, water, nail trimming or unfamiliar sensations. Zoomies will take reasonable precautions, including adjusting or stopping the groom where appropriate. However, minor accidental injuries can occur even where reasonable care is taken, particularly where a dog:

  • Moves suddenly;
  • Has severe matting;
  • Has loose or fragile skin;
  • Has warts, skin tags, lumps or hidden sores;
  • Is elderly or medically compromised;
  • Is anxious or aggressive; or
  • Has not been fully accustomed to grooming.

The customer accepts the inherent risks reasonably associated with grooming. Zoomies remains responsible to the extent required by law where an injury is caused by negligence or a failure to exercise reasonable care and skill.

15. Stopping or modifying a groom

The dog’s welfare takes priority over completing a particular style or service. Zoomies may stop, shorten or modify a groom where:

  • The dog becomes excessively distressed;
  • Continuing could cause pain or injury;
  • The dog displays aggression or presents a bite risk;
  • A medical or skin issue becomes apparent;
  • The coat condition is materially different from that disclosed; or
  • Completion cannot be achieved safely within reasonable handling limits.

The customer may be charged for the work and staff time already provided, even where the full planned groom cannot be completed. Sedation will not be administered by Zoomies.

16. Matted coats and shave-downs

Severe matting can cause pain, restrict movement, conceal skin conditions and increase the risk of irritation or accidental injury during removal. Where brushing out would cause unreasonable pain, distress or skin damage, Zoomies may recommend or carry out a short clip or shave-down, subject to customer consent where reasonably possible. Removing a tightly matted coat may reveal:

  • Redness or bruising;
  • Sores or wounds;
  • Parasites;
  • Skin infections;
  • Haematomas;
  • Warts, lumps or skin tags;
  • Irritation caused by reduced airflow; or
  • Conditions that were hidden beneath the coat.

Post-groom irritation, scratching, behavioural changes or sensitivity may occur after severe matting is removed. Zoomies will exercise reasonable care but is not responsible for a pre-existing condition merely because it becomes visible during or after grooming. Additional charges may apply for the time, equipment wear and specialist handling involved.

17. Nails and ears

Nail trimming will be carried out only so far as it can safely and reasonably be completed. The quick may be difficult to identify, particularly in dark, long or overgrown nails. Minor bleeding can occasionally occur despite reasonable care. Zoomies will take appropriate immediate steps if this happens. Zoomies provides routine external ear cleaning only. We do not diagnose or treat ear infections. Where pain, inflammation, odour, discharge or another concern is observed, veterinary examination may be recommended.

18. Pre-existing and underlying conditions

Grooming, excitement, exercise and group interaction may aggravate or reveal an existing condition, including arthritis, skin disease, heart or respiratory problems, seizures, anxiety or musculoskeletal injuries. The customer must disclose known or suspected conditions before attendance. Zoomies is not responsible for the natural progression or manifestation of a pre-existing condition unless it was worsened by a failure by Zoomies to exercise reasonable care.

19. Aggressive or difficult behaviour

Zoomies must be informed of any previous bite, snap, fight or aggressive incident. Where reasonably necessary, Zoomies may:

  • Use appropriate safety and handling equipment;
  • Apply a muzzle for a limited period;
  • Request assistance from another team member;
  • Modify or stop a service;
  • Separate the dog;
  • Require immediate collection; or
  • Refuse future bookings.

Any muzzle will only be used where reasonably considered necessary and will not be used as a substitute for appropriate welfare monitoring. An additional handling charge may apply where a dog requires materially more staff time than reasonably anticipated, provided the customer is informed as soon as reasonably practicable.

Dogs must arrive on a secure lead and with a properly fitted collar or harness. Customers should not leave valuable or irreplaceable belongings with their dog. Zoomies is not responsible for ordinary wear, damage or loss to leads, collars, harnesses, coats, bedding or toys arising from normal dog activity unless caused by Zoomies’ negligence.

  • Contact the dog’s usual veterinary practice;
  • Contact another available veterinary practice;
  • Transport or arrange transport for the dog; and
  • Authorise urgent veterinary assessment or treatment where delaying treatment to obtain the customer’s instructions could place the dog at risk.

Zoomies will make reasonable efforts to contact the customer and nominated emergency contact before non-routine treatment is authorised. The customer is responsible for reasonable veterinary and transport costs relating to illness, a pre- existing condition or an incident not caused by Zoomies’ negligence.
