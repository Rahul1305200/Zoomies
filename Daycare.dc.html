Questions

Daycare FAQs

What vaccinations are required?
For safety, all dogs must be up-to-date with:
  • DHPPi
  • Kennel Cough
Vaccination records must be provided before attendance.
Do dogs need to be neutered?
  • Female dogs over 1 year old must be neutered.
This helps maintain calm, balanced playgroups. If you’re unsure, just message us — we’re happy to advise.
What happens during the free trial?
Your dog receives:
  • A behaviour assessment
  • A free half day of daycare
  • Supervised play in small groups
  • Rest periods
  • A full update sent to you
There is no obligation to continue.
What if my dog hasn’t been to daycare before?
That’s exactly why we offer a free assessment and trial. We take introductions slowly and prioritise your dog’s comfort.
Do dogs go outside to toilet?
Many dogs naturally toilet indoors in daycare environments due to scent and routine changes. This is completely normal and managed by our team.
How are dogs grouped?
We group based on size, temperament and energy level. Groups are carefully balanced for safe, positive socialisation.
How many dogs are in each group?
We keep numbers controlled to maintain supervision and quality. We do not overcrowd.
