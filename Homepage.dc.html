Frequently Asked Questions

View All FAQs
How long does a grooming appointment take?

Typically 1.5–3 hours depending on coat type. We never rush an appointment — your dog’s comfort comes first.

Do you take puppies?

Yes. Our gentle puppy packages introduce your new puppy to the grooming environment and build positive experiences early.

Can you groom nervous dogs?

Of course. Our team are experienced with anxious and first-time dogs, and take all the extra time a dog needs.

What vaccinations are required for daycare?

Dogs need to be up to date with DHPPi and Kennel Cough. This protects every dog visiting the salon and the daycare room.
