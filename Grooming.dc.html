Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a groom take?

Typically 1.5–3 hours depending on coat type.

We never rush appointments — your dog’s comfort comes first.

What’s included in a full groom?

A full groom includes:

  • Bath & blow-dry
  • Brush-out
  • Trim (breed-standard or pet style)
  • Nail trim
  • Finishing & styling

We confirm everything before we begin.

What if my dog is nervous?

That’s completely normal.

Our team are experienced with anxious or first-time dogs and take extra time where needed.

Do you groom puppies?

Yes — we offer gentle puppy packages which are a great way to introduce your new puppy to the grooming environment and to build positive experiences early.

How often should my dog be groomed?

Most dogs benefit from grooming every 6–8 weeks.

Many clients pre-book to maintain coat health and condition.

Are vaccinations required?

We require dogs to be up-to-date with:

  • DHPPi
  • Kennel Cough

This helps protect all dogs visiting the salon.

Does my dog need to be neutered?

Neutering is not required for grooming appointments.

Can I stay with my dog during the groom?

While we completely understand the desire to stay close to your dog, we generally recommend that owners do not remain during the grooming session. In our experience, most dogs settle much more quickly and feel more relaxed when their owner is not waiting nearby.

Our experienced grooming team will ensure your dog is handled with care and comfort throughout the entire groom, and we will always keep you updated if needed.
